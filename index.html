Privacy Policy for Nháp

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Nháp is a note-taking application designed to let users create, edit, organize, and schedule reminders for their notes.

Information We Collect

Nháp does not operate a server and does not collect or transmit users' notes or personal information to the developer.

Notes, reminder information, and other app content created by the user are stored locally on the user's device.

Notifications and Reminders

If you choose to create a reminder, Nháp uses the device's local notification functionality to display the reminder at the scheduled time. Reminder information is stored locally on the device and is not sent to the developer.

Data Sharing

Nháp does not sell, rent, or share users' notes or personal information with third parties.

Accounts and Online Services

Nháp does not require an account and does not provide cloud synchronization or a developer-operated online storage service.

Advertising and Analytics

Nháp does not contain advertisements and does not intentionally use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services to collect user data.

Data Security

Because Nháp stores app content locally on the user's device and does not transmit that content to the developer, the security of locally stored information also depends on the security controls of the user's device.

Data Retention and Deletion

Nháp does not retain users' notes or reminder data on developer-operated servers. Local data remains on the device until the user deletes it, clears the app's data, or uninstalls the application, subject to the device's operating-system behavior and backups.

Children's Privacy

Nháp does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the application does not transmit user-created notes or personal information to the developer, no such information is knowingly collected by the developer through the app.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The updated policy will be published at the same public location with a revised "Last updated" date.
